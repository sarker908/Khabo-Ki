Find Food Near You

Search for a place in Bangladesh, or use your location, to see restaurants, cafés and fast food spots mapped on OpenStreetMap.

Searches start only when you press a button. We don't store your location. Its coordinates are sent to the free Overpass service to find nearby places, and place names you search for are sent to the free Nominatim service.

Map © Mapbox and © OpenStreetMap contributors. Places are found with Nominatim and the Overpass API from OpenStreetMap data. OpenStreetMap is edited by volunteers, so some places may be missing or out of date.
